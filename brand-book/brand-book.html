06 · Voice & tone

A chevruta, not a teacher.

We sit beside the viewer and read with them. Reverent without solemnity. Specific over generic. Plain English over rabbinic.

Pillars

Reverent without solemnity. Joy is welcome.
Specific over generic. Name the parsha, the aliyah.
Plain English over rabbinic. Translate; don't transliterate when English does the job.
Inclusive, not neutral. Same voice for the secular and the observant.

Pronouns & casing

Studiowe
Vieweryou
"I"attributed quotes only
Episode titlesTitle Case
UI labelsSentence case
Taglineslowercase OK

Tuning fork

✓ Like this

"Each week, we read the same story and meet someone new — ourselves."

"This week: Noach. The world ends. The world begins. A man plants a vine."

"12 minutes. One story. Posted Thursday."

✗ Not this

"Get ready for Torah like you've never seen it before!"

"Don't miss our latest drop on Parsha Noach!"

"Your weekly dose of Torah wisdom!"

If it can fade, fade.

Motion never bounces, never shimmies. The studio is patient enough to let the viewer think.
